Operating Model

Marketing should not support revenue. Marketing should create it.

Demand generation is not a campaign calendar. It is an operating discipline that connects market signal, account engagement, sales motion, and measurable pipeline outcome.

01
Pipeline-driven.
Plan every program with pipeline logic.

Clear account priority, conversion expectation, and commercial visibility before a single campaign launches. Quarterly and annual planning tied to pipeline contribution — not activity volume.

02
Revenue-first.
Translate investment into qualified outcomes.

Digital, event, ABM, social, content, and lead generation coordinated as one operating engine — not disconnected campaign activity.

03
Full-funnel ownership.
Manage the journey from signal to expansion.

Awareness through nurturing, opportunity creation, pipeline influence, and account expansion — with follow-up discipline and conversion tracking at every stage.
